TellyX User Guide & Help Center

Complete manual for IPTV playback, Timeshift, EPG & Shortcuts

📡 1. IPTV Playlist Connection
  • M3U Playlist URL: Enter your provider's M3U / M3U8 link and click Load Playlist.
  • Xtream Codes API: Input Server URL (e.g. http://server:8080), Username, and Password for automated live stream and VOD channel fetching.
  • Local M3U File: Choose a local .m3u or .m3u8 file from your disk.
⏪ 2. Live TV & Timeshift Playback
  • Live Edge Sync: Live TV streams display a live badge. Click LIVE to sync directly with real-time broadcast.
  • Timeshift Rewind & Seek: Drag the player seek slider or press ArrowLeft / ArrowRight to rewind up to 10 minutes in the buffer.
  • Return to Live Broadcast: When in Timeshift mode, click the GO LIVE ▶ badge on player controls to jump back to live transmission.
📅 3. TV Guide (EPG Matrix)
  • Click TV Guide (EPG) in the header or press G to open the interactive timeline matrix guide.
  • Click any program card in the schedule matrix to view show summaries and set program reminders.
